How every deal works

  1. 1. Choose online. Add materials to your cart, pick a stockpile offer, list what you sell, or send a sourcing request.
  2. 2. Finish on WhatsApp. Your request opens in a chat with Buildocrat, already filled in with a reference number.
  3. 3. Confirm and pay. We confirm stock, delivery cost and dates in writing, then send an invoice for a Buildocrat company account.
  4. 4. Delivered or held. We deliver to site, or hold your stockpile and deliver whenever you call it off.
